Stakeholder Validation

Identified Stakeholder Nicher and discussed opportunity for the system in the croissant market.

We regularly engaged with the stakeholder Nicher to explore potential opportunities for implementing our system in the croissant market. The stakeholder was enthusiastic about our proposed solution and shared our vision for its potential. Successfully printed Nicher's brand logo and the stakeholder found the results acceptable and gave feedback.

Pictured from left to right are Muslinmin, Kaushik, Reuben, Yugendren, Baker and Founder of Nicher: Melvin, and myself, Stafford.
