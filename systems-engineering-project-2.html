Systems Engineering Project 2

Define an Innovative Food Printing system using a Systems approach. The informal application of Systems Engineering Concepts & Principles based on 15288 Processes. The assignment started off with Concept Definition. The project team split up to conduct exploratory research and Domain Analysis and adopted agile methology with sprints as short as 1-3 days. This was crucial to prevent wasted work should any member of the project team go out of scope.
