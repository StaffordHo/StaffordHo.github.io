Systems Engineering Project 2 (SEP2) (Ongoing)

Define an Innovative Food Printing system using a Systems approach. The formal application of Systems Engineering (SE) Concepts & Principles based on 15288 Processes.

Learning Objectives

  • Apply academic knowledge of Systems Engineering with formal application of SE Processes in SEP2.
  • Practice Project Management skills to align the project team throughout the project and ensure timely deliverables.

Successfully demonstrated functionalities of prototype:

  1. Automation of print.
  2. Print on flat surfaces.
  3. Print on monochrome objects.
  4. Print with variability.

Identified Stakeholder Nichers and discussed opportunity for the system in the croissant market.

Stakeholder Validation

We engaged with the stakeholder Nicher to explore potential opportunities for implementing our system in the croissant market. The stakeholder was enthusiastic about our proposed solution and shared our vision for its potential.

Successfully printed Nicher's brand logo and the stakeholder found the results acceptable and gave feedback.
