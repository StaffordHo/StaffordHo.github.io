Collaborations: Bolloré Logistics

Collaborated with industry partner Bolloré Logistics in line with academic project: Project 4 to tackle problem statement: "Simulate assembly line automation. Using robotics and machines, the students can also model out before and after automation to show the productivity improvement and reduction of reliance on human resources."

Demonstration of prototype: Modular Assembly Line (MAL) verifying test cases

Upon being given the problem statement, we began analysing and refining the requirements for contradictions, ambiguities and incompleteness with the focus on the process of gathering requirements from stakeholders.

Then we went through a series of stakeholder engagements. First was on requirements refinement where we were given 5 questions to ask stakeholders and so our 5 questions are as follows:

  1. It was mentioned there was an existing solution: the variable end-effector robotic arm. And that solution was decomissioned due to the contractual period ending. Are there any other technical reasons why that solution was terminated? Is the variable end-effector unable to cope with the high mix low volume SKU?
  2. Can we have the general sizing dimensions of the SKUs from the smallest to the biggest and the general weight of SKUs from the lightest to the heaviest? What are some shapes of SKUs that we are dealing with?
  3. Is the entire VAS assembly line evenly bottleneck or only certain processes are bottlenecked? If the distribution is even, would overhaul be a possible solution?
  4. What is the floor layout/map of the VAS assembly line?
  5. Could we have some data on VAS assembly line for comparison and for future system requirements verification?
    • Input/Output rate of stations along the assembly line (To be compared when a new subsystem is integrated)
    • Peak output rate of assembly line (Current assembly system maximum capacity) (To be compared when a new subsystem is integrated)
    • Time study of stations/processes/human processes along the assembly line (Identify bottlenecks)
    • Motion study of processes/activities of workers along the assembly line (Identify motion waste)

2nd We pitched our proposed solution and they gave us the greenlight to begin prototyping.
