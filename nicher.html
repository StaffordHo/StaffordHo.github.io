Collaborations: Nicher

Identified an opportunity in the Croissant Market and collaborated with Nicher in line with academic project: Systems Engineering Project 2 (SEP2)

We regularly engaged with the stakeholder Nicher to explore potential opportunities for implementing our system in the croissant market.

The stakeholder was enthusiastic about our proposed solution and shared our vision for its potential.

Successfully printed Nicher's brand logo and the stakeholder found the results acceptable and gave feedback.
